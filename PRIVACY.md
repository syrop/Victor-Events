**What information do we collect?**

Unless you want to create or edit a community, or create or edit an event, you don't have to log in.

If you do log in, the program collects your Google e-mail address, display name, and user ID via Firebase Authentication, but you won't be contacted on your e-mail address. It collects information about the communities or events you create or edit, but it doesn't collect information on whether you attend a particular event.

If you grant location permission while creating or viewing an event on the map, approximate location data is used to center the map and display your position via Google Maps.

Whenever the application crashes, the program sends anonymous diagnostic information about the crash (such as stack traces, when it happened, your device model, the application version, and the version of the operating system you use) via Firebase Crashlytics.

**What do we use your information for?**

* Your Google account e-mail address is used solely to authenticate you and verify community administrator permissions. It is never used to send you marketing or unsolicited messages.
* Approximate location data (when permitted) is used only to display your position on the event map.
* Anonymous crash reports and diagnostics are used exclusively to identify bugs and improve the stability of the application.

**Data Retention and Deletion**

* **Communities and Events:** Community administrators can edit or delete their communities (along with associated events and administrator records) directly within the app at any time from the community details screen.
* **Account and Personal Data Deletion:** You can log out at any time from the app's menu. To request complete deletion of your authenticated account record and any associated e-mail entries from our Firebase backend without reinstalling the app, please open a deletion request issue at https://github.com/syrop/Victor-Events/issues or contact us via https://github.com/syrop. Requests are processed within 30 days.
